# Drone and energy-weapon HUD semantics (2026-10-08)

The carrier's DRONES action was assigned `StoreRole::bomb` solely to reuse the ground-point cross. The same shortcut covered gunship shells/cannon/gatling. The HUD used that role for its silhouette and bomb state, incorrectly treating deployed aircraft as dropped bombs. Special capabilities now use appended `gun`, `drone`, and `charge` roles; existing role IDs 0–3 remain stable. Their existing SpecialFrame aim/order points are independent of bomb-fall calculation. Only a non-null actual air/ground missile weapon asks the lock-target API. A drone has an aircraft silhouette and no bomb flag; its deployment/recall behavior is unchanged.

The Nix's normal laser rifles really use `EfsExposureBullet` (speed 10/frame, zero gravity, life 30), while DLC2 and laser cannon variants use `LaserBullet01`. Both were categorized as `arc` for flight prediction; vhud assumed every arc should receive a ballistic ladder and camera-target lead guides. Those are different concerns. `RoundModel::style` now identifies projectile/laser/beam/maser independently of `RoundKind`. Energy weapons preserve real-muzzle physical endpoints but have no projectile-drop ladder or ballistic lead guide. The HUD uses the energy-emitter silhouette and retains its energy label/range reticle without requiring fake ladder ticks.

The EMC's `V_510_MASER_THUNDER01.SGO` and both AI/mission variants declare `Weapon_VehicleMaser`, yet their AmmoClass is `SolidBullet01` (8/frame, zero gravity, life 75). Factory-only classification therefore mislabeled the atomic ray cannon as GUN. The supported native weapon vtable `0x17E5E40` is independently RTTI-checked as `.?AVWeapon_VehicleMaser@@`; this real weapon class takes precedence over the carrier bullet class and produces maser semantics and the localized atomic-ray label. This classifier does not claim that every energy weapon has an optical sight locator; the camera capability/position provider remains responsible for that.

Validation: real Root.cpk checks for nine energy weapons and file-read native RTTI checks; production ReadRound → Arm/GunMarkOf tests preserve ordinary ballistic guns while removing Nix/EMC ladders; special-role production checks, icon checks, complete four-language HUD layout checks, and offline rendering of carrier-drone/Nix/EMC fixtures. Complete plugin build uses /W4 /WX. No game execution or installation writes; live optics are not claimed tested.
